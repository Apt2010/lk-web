Due Diligence Guide

6 Things to Verify Before
Choosing a Builder

Before finalising your construction company, verify these critical factors to protect yourself from hidden costs, delays, and structural risks

01

Avoid Large Advance Payments

Never pay 3–5% of total project cost upfront. Payments should always be milestone-based. A design fee payment is acceptable, but nothing beyond that before work starts.

Financial Safety
02

Check Final Billing History

Speak to previous clients and confirm whether the final cost increased due to hidden or additional charges. Past clients are the most honest reference you have.

Due Diligence
03

In-House Architectural Team

Ensure the builder has an in-house architect for better coordination and fewer mistakes. External architects often lead to miscommunication and delays.

Team Structure
04

Structural Design After Soil Testing

Soil testing must be conducted before foundation design to ensure long-term safety. Foundation depth and column sizing should only be decided after proper soil analysis.

Technical Standards
05

Detailed Material Specifications

Avoid upfront structural promises without proper soil testing. Insist on detailed BOQ with specific brand and grade specifications for every material before signing.

Contract Clarity
06

Written Warranty Agreement

Make sure the warranty is real and documented in the contract — not promised verbally. The warranty must clearly state what is covered and for how long.

Legal Protection

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the construction cost per sq ft

LK Constraction offers house construction starting at ₹1899/sq.ft. The final cost depends on your plot size, number of floors, and finish level. Contact us for a detailed BOQ and free estimate tailored to your requirements.

How long does it take to build a house

A typical residential project takes 12 to 18 months. We provide a written project timeline before work begins with clear milestones so you always know exactly what happens next and when.

Do you handle complete construction from start to finish?

Yes. We offer complete turnkey house construction covering soil testing, architectural design, structural work, electrical, plumbing, finishing, and final handover — all under one roof with a dedicated site engineer throughout.

What areas do you cover?

We serve all major localities across Tamil Nadu including Anna Nagar, Peraiyur, Chinna Anuppanadi, Paravai, Melur, Othakadai, Palamedu, Alanganallur, Vadipatti, Tirumangalam, Alwarpuram, and surrounding areas.

Is your construction pricing transparent with no hidden costs?

Yes. We follow a milestone-based payment model with a detailed Bill of Quantities shared before work starts. You know exactly what you are paying for at every stage — no surprise charges, ever.

Do you assign a dedicated engineer to my project?

Yes. Every LK Constraction project gets a qualified, full-time site engineer assigned from day one. Your engineer is physically present on your plot every single day — monitoring material quality, workmanship, and progress at every stage.

What warranty do you offer on house construction?

LK Constraction provides a 15-year structural warranty on all residential construction projects. All warranty terms are documented in writing before the project begins — nothing is promised verbally.

Do you build G+1 and G+2 houses

Yes. We build single-floor independent houses, G+1, and G+2 residential homes across Tamil Nadu. Each project is structurally designed after soil testing — foundation depth and column sizing are always engineered, never assumed.
