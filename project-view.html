Project Overview

Sunset Grove Villas is a premium gated community of 8 luxury villas located in the heart of Anna Nagar, Madurai. Designed with modern architecture, VASTU compliance, and sustainable landscaping, each villa features 4 spacious bedrooms, a private garden, terrace deck, and smart home automation.

Key Features

  • VASTU compliant design
  • Smart home automation ready
  • Premium vitrified tiles & wooden flooring
  • Modular kitchen with chimney
  • Rainwater harvesting & solar provision
  • Landscaped garden & kids play area
  • 24/7 security & CCTV surveillance
  • 15-year structural warranty
